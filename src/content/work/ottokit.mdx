## Contributions

Product design, UX and UI, information architecture, product experience, marketing website design, responsive layouts, and WordPress website development using Astra and Spectra.

## About

OttoKit is a workflow automation platform for building multi-step flows across apps and integrations.

My work focused primarily on product design and the experience around the product. I also designed and developed OttoKit's marketing website inside WordPress using Astra and Spectra, translating the visual direction into a working website.

## Goals

The goal was to make OttoKit's product experience and website feel clear, approachable, and consistent while communicating the value of workflow automation.

- Shape a product experience that makes workflow automation easier to understand.
- Create clear structures for presenting product capabilities and workflows.
- Establish a consistent visual language across the product and website.
- Design a marketing website that communicates the product clearly without overwhelming visitors.
- Translate the design into a responsive, working WordPress website.

## Design Considerations

- **Make complex workflows easier to understand.** The product experience needed to communicate what users could do without relying on technical explanations alone.
- **Create a clear visual hierarchy.** Product capabilities, workflows, and supporting information needed to be presented in a way that was easy to scan.
- **Keep the product and website connected.** The marketing website needed to reflect the product's visual language and communicate its value accurately.
- **Design for responsive behavior.** Layouts, spacing, typography, and content structure needed to work across screen sizes.
- **Balance clarity with flexibility.** The experience needed to communicate the product's capabilities without making the interface or website feel unnecessarily complicated.
- **Design and implementation had to work together.** Building the website in WordPress using Astra and Spectra meant translating design decisions into the constraints and capabilities of the chosen tools.

> My work on OttoKit spanned product design and the development of its marketing website. I worked through the product experience, shaped the visual direction, and translated the design into a responsive WordPress website using Astra and Spectra.
